Background & Philosophy

Bridging Engineering Logic with Visual Storytelling.

I am a multidisciplinary developer and digital artist based in Sfax, Tunisia. Holding a Bachelor's degree in Big Data and Data Analysis from ISIMS, I combine technical software engineering with artistic craft.

As an AR & Software Developer at TDS (leading the MX-LAB industrial maintenance assistant) and former Multimedia Manager, I engineer spatial computing tools, build indie games in Unreal Engine 5, and direct commercial 3D CGI and video productions.

Whether simulating historical Roman turbines in 3D, writing interactive horror mechanics, or architecting responsive web applications, my goal is always to deliver memorable, intuitive experiences.
